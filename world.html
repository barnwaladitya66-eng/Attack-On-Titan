Paradis Military Branches

The Three Military Regiments

Following graduation from the Training Corps, recruits are assigned or choose between three distinct military branches based on merit and conviction.

Wings of Freedom

The Survey Corps (Scouts)

"Shinzo wo Sasageyo! (Dedicate your hearts!)"

The expeditionary arm of the military dedicated to venturing outside the Walls, reclaiming human territory, and engaging Titans in offensive 3D aerial combat.

Casualty Rate:

Over 70% per expedition prior to Year 850.

Notable Commanders: Erwin Smith, Levi Ackerman, Hange Zoë, Keith Shadis
Twin Roses

The Garrison Regiment

"The Shield of the People"

The largest military branch, tasked with maintaining and patrolling the 50-meter Wall defenses, operating heavy artillery, engineering fortifications, and preserving civil order during breaches.

Casualty Rate:

Moderate to High during active gate incursions.

Notable Commanders: Dot Pyxis, Hannes, Rico Brzenska, Kitz Woermann
Green Unicorn

Military Police Brigade

"In Service of the Crown"

The elite branch stationed within Wall Sina to guard the royal crown, collect taxes, manage civilian law, and maintain internal security. Historically corrupted and detached from frontline combat.

Casualty Rate:

Near 0% during peacetime; high privilege.

Notable Figures: Nile Dok, Kenny Ackerman, Hitch Dreyse, Boris Feulner

Fatal Target Specification

The Nape of the Neck (Cervical Spine)

Dimensions:

1 Meter (H) × 10 Centimeters (W)

Fatal Mechanism:

Direct excision of the human nervous coordinate prevents cellular regeneration.

Striking any other body part (limbs, throat, eyes) results in instantaneous steam generation and cellular regeneration within seconds. Only a clean, synchronized dual-blade diagonal slice across the 1m × 10cm nape will terminate a Pure Titan.
